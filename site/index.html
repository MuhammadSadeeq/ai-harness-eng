The eight wires

In turn order, one turn around the clock

    What is the model told, and which instruction wins?

    What happens next, what do we carry, when do we stop?

    Every run needs more than one exit.

    When something fails, how do we continue without spinning?

    Errors are the main path.

  5. Tools

    What can the model do, and is each tool easy to use?

    A tool is a user interface for a model.

  6. Permissions & sandbox

    Which actions are allowed, and how bad can a mistake get?

    Judge the action and its target.

  7. Verification & evals

    How do we know it's really done, and did the change help?

    “Done” is a claim.

  8. Observability

    What exactly happened, and where did time and money go?

    If it isn't in the trace, it didn't happen.
