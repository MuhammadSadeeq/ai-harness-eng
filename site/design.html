Design system

The surfaces, wires, glyphs, type, instruments and components the course is built from. Use the theme control in the top bar to see the day and night themes.

Surfaces and ink

Every colour comes from one token file. Each token has a day value and a night value. Night shift is a full theme: plates gain a visible border, and instrument windows stay dark in both themes.

  • --hx-paperpage background
  • --hx-panelplates and cards
  • --hx-panel-2insets, hover, table stripes
  • --hx-grid-minorfaint grid, inside instrument plates only
  • --hx-inktext and primary strokes
  • --hx-ink-2secondary text, margin notes
  • --hx-rulehairlines, plate borders
  • --hx-rule-strongform-control borders
  • --hx-focus3 px focus ring
  • --hx-instreadout windows, dark in both themes
  • --hx-inst-amberlive readout values
  • --hx-scrubinteractive-number highlight
  • --hx-code-bgcode and Wire JSON
  • --hx-passpass, always with a mark and a word
  • --hx-failfail, always with a mark and a word
  • --hx-warnwarnings

The eight wires

Each wire sits at a fixed clock angle in every view. Each wire has a glyph and a name, and its spoke sits at that angle.

  1. Control plane12 o'clock · 0° · solid 2 px · glyph layers
  2. Context & memory1–2 o'clock · 45° · long dash · glyph window
  3. Loop & state3 o'clock (the hand) · 90° · solid 3.5 px · glyph cycle
  4. Errors & recovery4–5 o'clock · 135° · dash-dot · glyph zigzag
  5. Tools6 o'clock · 180° · short dash · glyph hexagon
  6. Permissions & sandbox7–8 o'clock · 225° · dash-dot-dot · glyph diamond
  7. Verification & evals9–10 o'clock · 285° · dotted, round caps · glyph shield
  8. Observability11 o'clock (rim) · 330° · fine dash · glyph eye
